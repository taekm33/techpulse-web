Voice and agentic AI company <strong>SoundHound AI (Nasdaq: SOUN)</strong> closed its acquisition of <strong>LivePerson</strong> on September 4. The deal pairs SoundHound's voice-agent technology with LivePerson's enterprise digital messaging infrastructure to build an omnichannel customer-engagement platform spanning voice, web, mobile, SMS, and social. The combined company serves 25 of the Fortune 100, holds more than 750 patents, and enters life debt-free after SoundHound retired LivePerson's outstanding debt. Former LivePerson finance chief John Collins joins as the combined company's CFO.
</div>

Santa Clara-based SoundHound AI announced on September 4 that it had completed its acquisition of conversational AI company LivePerson. LivePerson stockholders approved the transaction on September 2, and with regulatory and shareholder clearances in place, the deal closed the same week. LivePerson common stock ceases trading on Nasdaq.

## What Got Combined — Voice Plus Digital Messaging

SoundHound is known for voice and agentic AI that operates across physical and digital channels — phones, kiosks, in-vehicle systems, smart devices, and more. LivePerson brings enterprise-scale digital messaging (chat and social) infrastructure. According to the company, LivePerson's platform will be integrated into **OASYS**, SoundHound's self-learning Orchestrated Agent System, to deliver a single conversational AI platform that runs natively across voice, web, mobile, SMS, and social channels.

Keyvan Mohajer, SoundHound's CEO and co-founder, called the merger "a defining moment for the new agentic AI era," saying global brands now have "a single, unified engine to power intelligent customer interactions." John Sabino, CEO of LivePerson, framed the combination as an "unparalleled value proposition for enterprises seeking to modernize their contact centers and digital touchpoints with a trusted, enterprise-grade AI partner."

## The Financials — A Debt-Free Reset and a New CFO

One of the closing's central themes is financial health. SoundHound says it retired LivePerson's outstanding debt as part of the close, leaving the combined company with a debt-free balance sheet. The company is targeting more than $500 million in future revenue from the existing customer base alone.

John Collins takes the CFO role at the combined company. A former founder, CFO, COO, and interim CEO at LivePerson, Collins previously led a turn from more than $100 million of annual cash burn to positive free cash flow in a single year, executed more than $200 million in cost reductions, and captured $227 million of debt discount through multi-year debt restructurings. He holds both a JD and an MBA, and SoundHound frames him as well-suited to drive the post-close financial integration, margin expansion, and cost discipline.

## Why Now — Aiming at the Agentic AI Market

SoundHound tied the combination to the fast-growing agentic AI market, citing a Gartner forecast that enterprise spend on the software will reach **$985 billion by 2030**. The logic behind the deal is demand to unify voice and digital channels into a single platform for contact centers and customer engagement.

For context, SoundHound posted record quarterly revenue of $61.9 million in Q2 2026 (up 45% year over year) and raised its full-year outlook.

## What It Means

The SoundHound-LivePerson combination is an attempt to put a voice leader and a digital-messaging leader under one roof, tracking the shift in conversational AI from standalone chatbots and voicebots toward omnichannel, agent orchestration. The open questions are the speed and quality of integration, and whether the debt-free reset actually translates into high-margin growth. With LivePerson delisting from Nasdaq, the two companies' independent histories close — and what remains is the combined company's ability to execute.
