## Two Years in the Making

Apple first hinted at a smarter Siri at WWDC 2024, but the rollout stumbled. On June 8, 2026, at the Worldwide Developers Conference, the company finally delivered. Craig Federighi, Apple's SVP of Software Engineering, called Siri AI "profoundly more intelligent, knowledgeable, and capable" — and the feature set backs that up.

Built entirely on Apple's new Apple Intelligence architecture, Siri AI is deeply embedded across iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and Apple Vision Pro. The launch covers six operating systems simultaneously: iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, visionOS 27, and tvOS 27.

## What's New in Siri AI

### Real-Time Web Knowledge
Siri AI can go out to the web for up-to-date information and synthesize answers on the fly. Combined with the ability to access your personal data — messages, emails, photos, calendar, contacts — Siri AI delivers responses that are contextually grounded in both current events and your personal life.

### On-Screen Awareness
Ask Siri AI about anything displayed on your screen and it understands. Reading a long article? Ask for a summary. Looking at a dense spreadsheet? Ask what stands out. No copying, no pasting, no context-switching. On macOS, Siri AI integrates directly into Spotlight, making it accessible from anywhere with a keystroke.

### Write with Siri
The new "Write with Siri" feature in Mail and Messages lets Siri AI draft text that mirrors how you normally communicate with that specific person. Sending a quick status update to your manager? It stays terse and professional. Texting a friend? It matches your casual tone. Siri AI can pull in details from the web, your past emails, calendar events, and contacts to build a relevant, accurate draft.

### Dedicated Siri App + iCloud Sync
For the first time, Siri gets its own standalone app. You can revisit past conversations, start new ones, and have full continuity across all your Apple devices. Conversation history syncs privately via iCloud — so a conversation started on iPhone seamlessly continues on Mac.

### New Voice Experience
Siri AI ships with a redesigned voice engine. Users can customize speaking pace and expressivity. System-wide dictation accuracy has been significantly improved — it now handles spelling, punctuation, and capitalization more reliably.

## What This Means for the AI Assistant Market

Siri AI's launch signals that Apple is no longer content to let OpenAI and Google define what an AI assistant should be. With over one billion active Apple devices in the world, even a modest adoption rate would instantly make Siri AI one of the most widely-used AI products on the planet.

The key differentiator Apple is betting on is privacy. Unlike ChatGPT or Gemini, which route data through external servers, Apple's on-device processing (via Apple Intelligence) means personal context — your messages, emails, photos — never has to leave your device for many operations. That's a meaningful pitch in enterprise and privacy-sensitive markets.
