<div class="article-tldr">
Alphabet CEO Sundar Pichai announced a leadership reshuffle at Google DeepMind (GDM) on August 5. Demis Hassabis is handing over day-to-day operations to become <strong>Chair of Google DeepMind and Chief Scientist of Alphabet</strong>, shifting his focus to long-term strategy and AGI. Former CTO <strong>Koray Kavukcuoglu</strong> takes over GDM's operations as Senior Vice President, reporting directly to Pichai. On the same day, <strong>Jeff Dean</strong> — who spent 27 years shaping Google's infrastructure and AI — is leaving to launch a new public benefit corporation called Discovery Loop.
</div>

Two figures who came to symbolize Google's AI both changed roles on the same day. In an internal message titled "The next chapter of our AI momentum," Pichai formalized a reorganization of Google DeepMind, bundling two major moves: Nobel laureate and DeepMind co-founder Demis Hassabis stepping back from CEO duties, and veteran engineer Jeff Dean departing after nearly three decades.

## Hassabis: "AGI feels close at hand"

Hassabis is handing over the "day-to-day operational responsibilities" he held as GDM CEO and moving into a dual role as Chair of Google DeepMind and Chief Scientist of Alphabet. Pichai said the change "allows him to put his full attention on actively shaping the future of AGI." Hassabis framed it as gaining "the time and space to focus on the big picture and help influence what is to come."

<blockquote>We have arrived at a pivotal moment in human history. I've been working towards AGI my whole life and now, like many of you, I feel it is close at hand.<br/>— Demis Hassabis</blockquote>

Hassabis will continue to work closely with Pichai on strategic and global AGI matters and advise Kavukcuoglu and the GDM leads. He also remains CEO of Isomorphic Labs, the DeepMind spin-off using AI to accelerate drug discovery, arguing that "the No. 1 application of AI should be to improve human health" — with curing diseases like cancer as the marquee test.

## Kavukcuoglu takes over GDM operations

Koray Kavukcuoglu, previously GDM's Chief Technology Officer, now becomes Senior Vice President reporting directly to Pichai. His remit spans <strong>Gemini model development, Frontier AI research, and the Gemini app and developer teams</strong>.

Pichai noted that Kavukcuoglu "has been at DeepMind since its early days, and over his 13 years there he started our deep learning team and led breakthroughs like WaveNet and DQN." Hassabis added that "the Gemini models are in good hands," pointing to progress on the next-generation Gemini 4.

## Jeff Dean's exit and Discovery Loop

The other axis of the announcement is the departure of Jeff Dean, who spent 27 years architecting Google's core infrastructure and AI. Dean is founding Discovery Loop, an independent public benefit corporation aimed at accelerating discoveries in machine learning, science, and engineering. Google is a founding investor and Cloud partner and plans to collaborate on a research framework for ML systems and related infrastructure. Gemini co-lead Oriol Vinyals is also leaving Google to join Dean.

<div class="article-callout info">
The movement of key researchers tends to affect long-term model roadmaps more than near-term results. This reshuffle looks less like an exodus and more like a structural split of "operations vs. long-horizon research" — but the simultaneous exits of Dean and Vinyals still signal a shifting talent landscape.
</div>

## Why now

The reorganization reads as an effort to make Google's AI org leaner in its frontier-model race with Anthropic and OpenAI. It frees Hassabis to concentrate on long-term research and AGI strategy while consolidating product-facing Gemini development and deployment under a single leader. Coming amid months of high-profile AI talent movement around Google, the open question is how much this sharpens Google's execution.

<div class="article-callout tip">
When reading org changes, reporting lines and scope of ownership tell you more than titles. Here the real signal is the simplification of the chain of command: Gemini development, Frontier research, and the app all roll up to one SVP reporting straight to Pichai.
</div>
