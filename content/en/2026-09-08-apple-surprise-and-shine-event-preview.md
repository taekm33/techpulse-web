Apple's fall season is one day away. On August 26, Apple officially teased a special event under the tagline "Surprise and Shine." The event takes place at 10:00 a.m. Pacific Time on September 9 at the Apple Park campus and will be streamed online. The fact that the event is happening — along with its time, place, and name — is information Apple confirmed directly; the specific products on stage remain in the realm of expectation until the keynote. Below, we clearly separate what is confirmed from what is reported and rumored.

## Confirmed: The event itself and the Macs announced early

What Apple has made official is the event. September 9, 10:00 a.m. PT, Apple Park, "Surprise and Shine." Select members of the media are invited to attend in person. Apple's September events have traditionally centered on that year's iPhone lineup alongside new Apple Watch models.

Some products have already been announced ahead of the show. On August 25, Apple unveiled a new Mac mini (with the M6 or M5 Pro chip) and Mac Studio (with M5 Max and M5 Ultra chips); both are available to pre-order now and ship on September 22. In other words, the spotlight tomorrow is likely to be on the iPhone and wearables rather than the Mac.

## The expected stars: iPhone 18 Pro and the foldable 'iPhone Ultra'

The biggest point of interest is Apple's first foldable iPhone. According to multiple reports, it is referred to internally as "iPhone Ultra." Cited strengths include a folded form factor that fits comfortably in a pocket, a durable hinge mechanism, and iPad-style app layouts that take advantage of the larger internal display when unfolded. Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reported that early testers were largely positive, though the camera setup — which reportedly lacks a telephoto lens — drew some criticism. Several outlets expect the price to exceed $2,000.

The iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max are also expected to appear. This year Apple is reportedly splitting its lineup: the Pro models and the foldable arrive in the fall, while the more affordable iPhone 18 is pushed to spring 2027 — a "split launch." The Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 are expected to share the same stage.

## Why this event matters

This event is symbolic for two reasons. First, after years of rumors, Apple is expected to enter the foldable market for the first time. Joining a category that Samsung, Huawei, and others have cultivated for years, Apple arrives late — so its execution and pricing will be closely watched for their impact on the market. Second, this is effectively the first major product event under John Ternus, who took over as CEO from Tim Cook on September 1. With a hardware engineer now leading the company, how Apple has solved the hardware challenge of a folding form factor could become an early yardstick for his tenure.
